kyo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

kyo is a personal AI companion you talk to over text messages, Instagram direct messages, and the kyo app. This policy explains what data kyo handles and how.

What we collect

  • Messages and content you send kyo — texts, photos, links, and Instagram reels or posts you share with kyo's account.
  • Derived memory — facts and context kyo learns from your conversations so it can remember and be useful to you over time.
  • Account identifiers — your phone number, and (if you connect Instagram) your Instagram user ID, used only to link your messages to your own private memory.
  • Device tokens — if you use the kyo app and enable notifications.

How we use it

Only to provide kyo: responding to you, remembering what you've shared, and sending you the messages you've asked for. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared across users. Each person's memory is isolated and private.

Processors

To operate, kyo relies on a small set of service providers: message delivery providers (for text messaging), Meta's Instagram platform (for Instagram messages), Apple (for app notifications), and AI model providers that process message content to generate kyo's replies. These providers process data only to deliver the service.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — see how to delete your data. Deletion removes both the raw content and everything kyo derived from it.

Instagram

If you message kyo's Instagram account, kyo receives the messages and shares you send to that account (via Meta's Instagram API). kyo does not access your profile, your followers, your feed, or anything beyond the direct-message conversation you have with kyo.
