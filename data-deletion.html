kyo — Delete Your Data

Last updated: July 23, 2026

You can delete your kyo account and everything kyo knows about you at any time. Deletion is total: your messages, shared content, and all memory kyo derived from them are permanently removed.

How to delete

  • In the kyo app — account settings → delete account.
  • By message — text kyo and ask it to delete your account.
  • By email — send a request to justin04cheng@gmail.com from the contact information linked to your account.

Instagram data

If you've messaged kyo's Instagram account, deleting your kyo account also removes the link between your Instagram ID and your memory, plus anything captured from your Instagram messages. You can additionally remove kyo's app access at Instagram → Apps and Websites.

Timing

Deletion takes effect immediately upon confirmation. There are no residual copies kept for advertising or analytics — kyo does neither.
